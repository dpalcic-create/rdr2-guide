Start Here: Epilogue To-Do

Playing as John after the story? This is a suggested order that front-loads Epilogue-only content. Tap any tab for the full how-to.

    Epilogue facts worth knowing

    • New Austin opens in the Epilogue: 3 legendary animals, 2 legendary fish, 8 dinosaur bones, the Elemental Trail and Otis Miller's treasures, and the Del Lobo hideouts.
    • The 9 graves for Paying Respects can only all be visited after the story.
    • The American Inferno, Burnt Out is the only John-exclusive stranger strand.
    • The Strange Man's portrait at Bayall Edge only finishes as John after the Epilogue.
    • Most bounty posters for John are in Tumbleweed and Blackwater after Gainful Employment.

    Legendary Animals

    Take pelts to the Trapper (Saint Denis is the main shop). Several items need two legendary pelts, so check the crafts before you ride out.

    How legendary hunts work

    1. Unlock the mapFinish Hosea's Chapter 2 mission Exit Pursued by a Bruised Ego to get the legendary animal map.
    2. Enter the zoneRide into the marked area. If it says there's too much activity, finish the nearby event and come back.
    3. Follow clues with Eagle EyeInspect three clues (the Bear needs two); the last one leads you to the animal.
    4. Kill it any way you likeA legendary always gives a perfect pelt, so bring your strongest rifle, Express ammo and full Dead Eye.
    Legendaries don't respawn. If the body is lost (in water, off a cliff), the materials are sent to the Trapper and Fence automatically. The Panther only appears once Master Hunter challenges 1 to 9 are done; killing it is challenge 10.

    Legendary Fish

    1. Start A Fisher of FishAfter the story mission A Fisher of Men, meet Jeremy Gill on the pier on Flat Iron Lake's northeast shore. He gives you the legendary fish map.
    2. Buy the right lureEach fish needs its Special Lake, River or Swamp Lure (listed on each card).
    3. Catch and mailMail each catch to Jeremy from a post office. Rewards: Lake Lure (1), $45 and 5 Succulent Fish Meat (10), invitation (13).
    4. Finale (Epilogue)Meet him in Rio Bravo to photograph him landing a giant catfish. Take his hat (missable) and the Special Spinner.

    Treasure Maps

    Sell gold bars to any Fence for $500 each. The four main hunts alone pay 15 bars. Finding treasures also advances the Explorer challenges.

    Stranger Missions

    Any 10 stranger strands count toward 100% and the Western Stranger trophy. Strands marked Epilogue OK were confirmed playable as John; the rest weren't confirmed either way.

    Collectibles

    New Austin and the Epilogue

    New Austin (Tumbleweed, Armadillo, Rio Bravo, Cholla Springs, Gaptooth Ridge, Hennigan's Stead) is only reachable in the Epilogue. Everything here shares its found-it ticks with the other tabs.

    Challenges

    90 challenges in 9 sets of 10. Each set appears after its first action (shown below), steps unlock in order, and progress only counts once a step is active. Rewarded gear (holsters, bandoliers, belts) is bought from the Trapper. All nine sets unlock the Legend of the East outfit, and all 90 are needed for 100%.

    Tips and Tricks

    Money early

    • Gold bars sell for $500 each at a Fence: do the Jack Hall, High Stakes, Poisonous Trail and Landmarks hunts early.
    • Free bars: one in the Limpany sheriff's office (south of Valentine), two in the upended car of the train wreck near Cotorra Springs.
    • Three more in the strange statues cave by the Mysterious Hill Home (statues with 2, 3, 5, 7 fingers; the armless one has 7).
    • Rob the Strawberry general store's basement still for a $50 wall stash.

    Camp ledger

    • The ledger unlocks after Money Lending and Other Sins II in Chapter 2.
    • Buy Leather Working Tools ($225) for Pearson's satchels.
    • Buy Dutch's lodging ($220), then Arthur's lodging ($325) for the fast travel map in your tent.
    • Horse Station ($300) lets you swap or call horses at camp.

    Only 3-star animals give perfect pelts. Check the animal's info first and land one clean head or vital shot.

    Horses

    • Tame the free White Arabian near Lake Isabella, one of the best horses in the game. Save before you approach, then ride her to a stable.
    • Max your bond before Horseman challenges; petting restores stamina based on bond level.
    • The Rolling Block Rifle from Chapter 2's Pouring Forth Oil is ideal for legendaries.

    Cores and Dead Eye

    • Completed challenge sets raise a core and your ammo capacity.
    • Save tonics for hard fights; chewing tobacco and moonshine cover routine top-ups.
    • At Dead Eye level 4 you can see animal weak points.

    General

    • Do white Stranger missions before yellow story missions; some white ones disappear as the story moves.
    • Wear a bandana for robberies and swap outfits after.
    • Don't pick up worn weapons; they stay on your horse forever.
    • Stuck somewhere? Set camp to warp you and your horse to safety.

    Cheat Codes

    Any active cheat disables saving and achievements. Enter them at the end of a session from Settings, then Cheats. Type them exactly, punctuation included.
